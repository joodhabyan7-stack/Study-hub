📕 Eleven

By Sandra Cisneros

📚 Eleven — Study

📝 Summary

Rachel is turning eleven. She realizes that growing older does not make her younger selves disappear. When Mrs. Price makes her wear a red sweater that Rachel says is not hers, Rachel becomes embarrassed and upset.

🎯 Main Theme

Growing older does not erase your past experiences or the younger parts of yourself.

👤 Rachel

Rachel is thoughtful and sensitive. She feels frustrated because she knows the sweater is not hers, but her teacher does not listen to her.

👩‍🏫 Mrs. Price

Mrs. Price is Rachel's teacher. She incorrectly believes that the red sweater belongs to Rachel and makes Rachel put it on.

👕 The Red Sweater

The sweater represents Rachel's embarrassment, frustration, and feeling of being misunderstood.

🔍 Figurative Language

Simile: Rachel compares the sweater to a big red mountain.

Simile: The sweater hanging from the desk is compared to a waterfall.

Rachel's younger ages represent the idea that her past remains part of who she is.
